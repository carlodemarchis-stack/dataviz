Formula 1 · F1viz

A season read lap by lap

The 2026 championship in three films — Drivers, Constructors and Races. Every Grand Prix opens into a deep dive: the position trace with its pit windows and safety cars, lap-time pace, gap to the leader, gap to the car ahead — and a self-playing timing tower where all twenty-two cars re-sort themselves on every lap.

Tennis · ATP & WTA

The season, one player at a time

Two hundred cards in ranking order — the ATP Top 100 and the WTA Top 100, each on their own full-screen page. Every tournament played, every set score, head-to-heads against the rivals who actually mattered, and the money charts at the end. Then step across to the draws, where a finished tournament unfolds as a fan of matches.

World Cup 2026

Road to the Final

The entire knockout tournament as a single radial bracket — every nation, every score, every penalty shoot-out in one picture. Tap a match for goals, cards, line-ups and possession, or press ▶ Film and let the tournament replay itself beat by beat. You can also follow one nation from their first group game to the final whistle, and dig into the Controversy Tracker for the calls that decided it.

NFL Season Tower

All thirty-two teams as towers on a shared baseline: wins stack upwards, losses hang below, and the games still to come drop from the ceiling. Scrub through the season and watch the standings re-order themselves. A Season Film gives every franchise its own card.

Season Tower — Europe's top five

The same ledger applied to football, for Serie A, the Premier League, La Liga, Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga. Every point won is drawn above the line, every point dropped below it — so a season becomes a shape you can read at a glance.
